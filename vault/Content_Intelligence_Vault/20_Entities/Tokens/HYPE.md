# HYPE

The native token of the Hyperliquid ecosystem. Across the captured sources HYPE appears as an **alignment mechanism** and as a **collateral asset**, rather than being defined in detail.

## What the sources say

- The Almanack lists "HYPE alignment" as one of Hyperliquid's differentiating pillars ([[the-almanack-of-hyperliquid]]).
- In the HyperEVM landscape, HYPE (and liquid-staking forms like kHYPE / StakedHYPE) is a collateral asset — e.g. Derive's bridge vault makes HYPE/kHYPE usable as collateral for options and perps ([[what-should-exist-on-hyperevm-four-pillars]]).
- Adjacent signal (not primary): Multicoin published a HYPE valuation and holds it as one of their largest liquid-fund positions (from the sidebar of [[the-almanack-of-hyperliquid]]).
