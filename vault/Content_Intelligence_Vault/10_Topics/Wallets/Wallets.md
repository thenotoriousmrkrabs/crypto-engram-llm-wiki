# Wallets

Wallet architecture and strategy, with a current emphasis on wallets as the authorization layer for AI agents.

## Current understanding

- This is the same need surfacing from the agent side: agents moving to production require "wallet-like authorization" ([[ai-agents-demos-to-production]]). The two topics meet at agent-scoped permissions and policy — see [[AI_Agents]].
